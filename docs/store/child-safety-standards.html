KidsPool Child Safety Standards

KidsPool app, published by NFC Solutions · Last updated: 16 November 2026

KidsPool has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any such content or behavior is strictly prohibited in the KidsPool app. We remove it, ban the accounts involved, and report it to the authorities.

About KidsPool

KidsPool helps parents and legal guardians share school and activity rides with other verified families nearby. Only adults (18+) can create an account. Children do not use the app and cannot hold accounts. Parents add their children's details only to arrange rides.

What is prohibited

On KidsPool, nobody may:

  • Create, upload, share, request or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, edited or AI-generated material.
  • Groom, solicit, sexualize, sextort or otherwise sexually exploit a child, or try to arrange contact with a child for any sexual purpose.
  • Use rides, chats, profiles, photos or children's information (names, schools, activities, locations, schedules) to target, track, contact or harm a child.
  • Traffic, or facilitate the trafficking of, a child for any purpose.
  • Share sexual, violent or otherwise inappropriate content involving or directed at minors.
  • Pretend to be a parent, guardian or driver, or use false identity documents, to get access to children.

How we keep children safe

  • Adults only: every account belongs to an adult parent, guardian or driver. Drivers must verify a government-issued driver's license before giving rides.
  • Limited sharing: other families see only a first name, an approximate area and the school or activity. Exact home addresses and phone numbers stay private until a ride is booked between two parents.
  • Ride tracking: parents can follow a ride live from start to drop-off, and each ride ends with a drop-off confirmation.
  • Emergency tools: an SOS button during rides alerts the other adult and KidsPool, alongside a prompt to call local emergency services.
  • Report and block: any parent can report or block another user from chats, group member lists and family cards. A blocked person's messages and family card are hidden right away.
  • Moderation: we review every report, remove violating content, and permanently ban accounts that break these standards.
  • Account deletion: users can permanently delete their account and data from Profile → Delete account.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: long-press a message or a family card, or open a ride chat's ⋮ menu or a group member, and tap Report. You can block the person at the same time. Reports go straight to the KidsPool safety team.
  • By email: bowapps@nfcsolutionsusa.com. Include the account name and the ride or chat involved, and what happened. Don't send images of abuse; describe them instead.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in India) first.

We aim to review child safety reports within 24 hours.

How we respond

  • We remove CSAE content and suspend the accounts involved as soon as we're aware of them.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national and local authorities. In India, that includes the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • We keep the relevant records and share them with law enforcement as the law requires.
  • Accounts banned for CSAE are not allowed back.

Compliance with the law

KidsPool complies with child safety laws in every country where the app is available. That includes the obligation to report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities.
