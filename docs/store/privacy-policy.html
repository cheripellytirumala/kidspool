KidsPool: School Ride Sharing – Privacy Policy

App: KidsPool: School Ride Sharing (Google Play, com.nfc.kidpool) · Developer: NFC Solutions · Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short: KidsPool helps parents and legal guardians share school and activity rides with other families nearby. We collect the information needed to match families and run rides safely: your phone number, your name, your kids' details, your addresses, your location, and your ID or driver's license checks. Other families see only limited details. We don't sell your data and don't use it for advertising. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

1. Who we are

KidsPool is operated by NFC Solutions ("we", "us"). We decide how your personal data is used in the app. Contact details are in section 13.

KidsPool is for adults (18+) only. Children don't use the app or have accounts. Parents add information about their children so that rides can be arranged.

3. What other users can see

  • Families near you see your first name and last initial, an approximate distance, whether you give rides, and the school or activity you share. They don't see your exact address, phone number or ID documents.
  • When a ride is booked between two parents, they can see the pick-up and drop-off addresses, the kids on the ride, the phone number for calling, and the live car location while the ride is under way.
  • Members of a ride group see the messages posted in that group and the members' first names and kids' first names.

4. How we share information

We don't sell personal data and don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • With other users, as described in section 3.
  • With service providers who process data for us under contract:
    • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in hosting.
    • Google Maps Platform: maps and place search (receives search text and map locations).
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications (receives a device token).
    • An SMS provider: sends one-time sign-in codes (receives your phone number).
  • For safety and legal reasons: with law enforcement or authorities when required by law, or to protect a child or anyone from harm. That includes reporting child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to Indian authorities.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, under this policy.

5. Location data

  • We ask for location permission before using it. You can say no and type addresses instead.
  • Background location is used only for the driving parent, only during an active ride, with an ongoing notification on Android, so the other parent can follow the car until drop-off.
  • You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings. Live ride tracking then won't work.

6. Children's information

KidsPool isn't directed at children, and children can't create accounts. A parent or legal guardian adds and controls their children's information, and confirms that they have the right to do so. Only the information needed to arrange rides is shown to other families: first names, school or activity, and grade. Health notes and emergency contacts are shown only to the parent who is driving that child, and only while that ride is booked or under way. You can edit or delete a child's profile at any time in the app.

Child safety: child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) is prohibited

KidsPool: School Ride Sharing, by NFC Solutions, strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking. We remove such content, permanently ban the accounts involved, and report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the relevant authorities. Anyone can report a concern in the app (Report on any chat, family card or group member) or by email to kiran@nfcsolutionsusa.com, our child safety point of contact. Our full Child Safety Standards explain the rules and how we enforce them.

7. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account data while your account is open.
  • Live ride locations are used during the ride. The ride record (route summary, times) is kept with your ride history until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, children, addresses, ride requests, chats, group memberships, ID and license photos and other files, generally right away and in all cases within 30 days. Messages you sent in group chats stay visible to the group, without your name.
  • Safety reports and records of SOS events or policy violations may be kept for as long as needed for safety reviews, legal claims, or as the law requires.

8. Deleting your account

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • By email: write to kiran@nfcsolutionsusa.com from anywhere, with the phone number on your account. We'll verify it's you and delete the account.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to it is restricted by account-level security rules. ID and license photos are kept in private storage and are not visible to other users. No system is completely secure. Please tell us right away if you think your account has been misused.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent and nominate someone to act for you. Most of this can be done in the app (Profile). For anything else, email us (section 13). We'll respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, for example on cloud servers in other regions. When they do, we use contractual and technical safeguards to protect it.

12. Changes to this policy

We'll post any change here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
