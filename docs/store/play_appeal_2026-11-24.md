# Google Play appeal — KidsPool: School Ride Sharing (com.nfc.kidpool)

Submit from **Play Console → Policy status (Policy center) → the suspension → Appeal**.
Do not use the "Submit an appeal" link in the email: it points to a different
app id (`com.dima.marlo`).

Appeal text (paste as is; ~2,000 characters):

---

Hello Google Play team,

We are appealing the suspension of KidsPool: School Ride Sharing (com.nfc.kidpool). The earlier rejections were about our Child Safety Standards and privacy policy declarations. We have reviewed every previous rejection notice and fixed the causes:

1. Developer name now matches our Play account. Our privacy policy and Child Safety Standards previously named a trading name ("NFC Solutions") instead of our registered developer name. Both pages now name Coiffure Tek Solutions Pvt Ltd as the developer, together with the exact app name "KidsPool: School Ride Sharing" and package com.nfc.kidpool.

2. Child Safety Standards (https://kidspool-legal.netlify.app/child-safety/) is a public, globally accessible page that explicitly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and CSAM, explains in-app reporting (report on any chat, family card or group member) and email reporting, states that we report CSAM to NCMEC and relevant authorities, and names a child safety point of contact (kiran@nfcsolutionsusa.com) who can speak to our CSAM prevention practices.

3. Privacy policy (https://kidspool-legal.netlify.app/privacy/) is public, not geofenced, not a PDF, and covers what we collect (phone number, name, home and school location, children's first names and photos added by their parent, driver's license images for verification, live location during a ride), why, retention, deletion and contact details.

4. In-app account deletion is available (Profile → Delete account) and removes the account and its data.

5. Play Console declarations were updated to match the app: Data safety form, Child Safety Standards URL and contact, Privacy policy URL, target audience (adults: parents and guardians; the app is not directed at children).

KidsPool is used only by verified adult parents and guardians to share school rides. Children never sign in or use the app. Drivers verify a driver's license, live location is shared only during a ride and only with that ride's parent, and every chat and profile can be reported or blocked.

We have also reviewed the other apps in our developer account for compliance. We are committed to following the Developer Program Policies and ask you to reinstate the app.

Thank you,
Coiffure Tek Solutions Pvt Ltd
kiran@nfcsolutionsusa.com
